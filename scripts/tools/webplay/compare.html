Pick two models and deal.
Spymaster view: every card's team is shown. Faded cards were already revealed. L/R badges mark the own words each clue is meant for.
Boards are dealt at random with 0–8 cards already revealed, and dealt past when the two models give the same clue and number. Meant for is the clue's number of own words, taken from that model's own listener: the ones it scores highest for the clue. First guess is that listener's probability that a guesser's first pick is each word. Pass is the model's outside option, when it has one. Named is the association models' predicted share of free-association lists that name the word. Expected is that model's expected net words for the clue. In blind mode, names and probabilities stay on the server until you vote. Votes are appended to cache/compare_votes.jsonl. k=1 tiebreak: __K1__.
